About this Bible

Versions

  • KJV: King James Version (1769) with Strong’s numbers and the translators’ italics for supplied words.
  • BSB: Berean Standard Bible, public domain since 2023, with Strong’s from the Berean interlinear tables (bereanbible.com). It is the closest free version to the NKJV; the NKJV itself is copyrighted and can’t be included.
  • WEB: World English Bible, British Edition, public domain.
  • ASV: American Standard Version (1901), public domain. Where it prints “Jehovah”, this Bible shows “LORD”, as the KJV does.
  • BLB: Berean Literal Bible, the word-for-word companion to the BSB, public domain. Words in italics are supplied by the translators, “YHWH” is shown as “LORD”, and ⁺ marks a plural “you”.

Red letters and God’s words only are available in the KJV, BSB and WEB. Strong’s is available in the KJV and BSB: underlined words open their Hebrew or Greek entry. The NKJV isn’t included because it’s copyrighted; the version menu opens it, with Strong’s, on Blue Letter Bible, or you can import a copy you own (☰ → Import a Bible).

God’s words only

Shows only speech the text directly attributes to God, Jesus or the Holy Spirit, and hides the narration around it. It is built from Clear Bible’s speaker data, which marks quoted speech. In the prophets it also follows the Lord’s longer quotations across verses, using markers like “declares the LORD”, and carries these over to the KJV where the BSB and WEB agree. Passages where the prophet speaks for God without any quotation marks may still be only partly included, and a few verses with two speakers may keep a line from the other speaker.

Shortcuts

Tap a verse number to highlight it, add a note, copy it or see cross references (underlined words open their Strong’s entry). Tap the title at the top, or press ⌘K, to choose a book and chapter, search, or type a reference like “Ps 23” or “1 Cor 13:4”. Change chapter with ← → or the arrows at the page edges, or swipe on a phone. The dice picks something to read; ☰ has the reading plan, highlights and Bible import. Esc closes panels.
